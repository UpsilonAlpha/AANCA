# The four frozen arms show the architecture cannot place residues from local information

**Held implicitly all day on 6 Nov and never stated plainly, which is how it
survived.** Each arm's write-up concluded something narrower -- about FAPE,
the clamp, the trunk -- while the shared unstated premise was that
whole-structure reconstruction from a baseline *is* the task, so failing at it
is the rule failing.

It is not the task. The project's objective is regeneration from damage, and no
arm has trained on it
([[whole-structure-growth-is-a-harder-and-different-task-than-regeneration]]).
Reconstruction with native context at one residue in eight is strictly harder
than filling a window into intact surroundings, and each mechanism blamed today
-- the clamp's live fraction, the 36 A reach ceiling, the trivial fixed point
-- weakens or disappears once the context is present.

What the arms do establish stands and should be carried forward: the rule holds
still under an objective with no usable gradient, identity trains normally over
the same updates, and the bond term degrades wherever identity is supervised.
Those are findings about the loss and about the identity term. They are not a
verdict on the architecture, and reading them as one would have retired a
design on evidence from a task it was not built for.
