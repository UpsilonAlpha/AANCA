# The orientation term buys about 22 degrees of twist against a run without it

**Committed as a finding on the morning of 6 Nov and corrected within the
hour.** Kept as the canonical example of the error this map exists to prevent.

The claim compared `g5_twist`'s 68.5 deg against 90.2 deg "a run without it
reaches". The 90.2 came from **g4, on a different crop sample**. Crop-matched
probing put the three g5 arms at 65.5, 67.1 and 68.5 -- so the orientation term
is the *worst* of the three on twist, on every window, and on global RMSD,
rather than 22 degrees better than a control.

Why the number was not comparable: see
[[a-collapsed-rules-twist-cannot-be-compared-across-crop-samples]].

What survives from that morning is the opposite ordering, and it is still held:
rank the arms by how far they sit from the helical initialisation (6.7, 11.3,
16.5 deg) and twist error rises monotonically (65.5, 67.1, 68.5). Every
departure from the single conformation has so far been noise or worse.
