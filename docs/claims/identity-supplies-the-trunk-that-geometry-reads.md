# Identity is not geometry's competitor for the trunk; it is geometry's supplier

Two facts that only mean something together.

Identity's gradient dominates the shared trunk by 2,350x on `g6_self`
([[identity-dominates-the-shared-trunk-by-2350x]]) -- exactly the number a
starvation hypothesis would predict. And removing identity does not free the
geometry: `g6_noid` is *more* static than `g6_self`, perfectly so
([[g6-noid-never-left-the-helix-baseline]]).

So the trunk representation is identity's, bought by the only term whose
gradient is large enough to shape it, and the frame head reads that
representation. Take identity away and the frame head is reading a trunk that
nothing is training.

This matters for what a fix may be. A 2,350x ratio invites a reweighting, and
`g6_noid` is the experiment showing a reweighting cannot work: the small term's
problem is not that the large one crowds it out.

**On check 1's finding that every observation beneath this is `ours`:** it is,
and it stands. The claim is *about* our own model's gradient flow -- which
parameters a term's gradient reaches -- so a measurement of our own model is
the appropriate evidence rather than a weaker substitute for an experimental
one. The check's rationale, that a calculation on an input is evidence about
the calculation, is exactly why this qualifies: the calculation is the subject.
